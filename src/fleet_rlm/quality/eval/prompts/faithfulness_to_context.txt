- Does the answer reflect information actually present in the context?
- Are claims in the answer supported by the provided context?

- 0.7-0.9: Answer is mostly faithful with minor unsupported claims
- 0.4-0.6: Answer has significant portions not grounded in context
- 0.1-0.3: Answer is largely hallucinated with few grounded claims
- 0.0: Answer is completely fabricated and not grounded in context

You must respond with a single float value in the closed interval [0.0, 1.0] representing the faithfulness score.
Do not include any explanation, reasoning, or additional text in your response.
Respond only with the numeric score.
